# Key Insights & Recommendations

## Key Insights

- The dataset contains approximately 3.48 million rides.
- Annual members recorded more rides overall than casual riders.
- Casual riders had a much higher average ride duration than annual members.
- Casual rider activity was particularly strong on weekends.
- July and August showed high casual rider activity.
- Docked bikes had the highest overall usage.
- Casual riders using docked bikes had longer average ride durations than annual members.

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Weekend-focused Membership Promotions
Launch membership promotions targeted at casual riders during weekends.

### 2. Target Long-duration Casual Riders
Identify casual riders with longer ride durations and target them with membership offers.

### 3. Seasonal Marketing Campaigns
Increase digital marketing efforts during July–August when casual rider activity is high.

### 4. Limited-time Membership Offers
Offer limited-time membership trials, discounts, or promotional incentives to encourage casual riders to become annual members.

## Conclusion

The analysis shows a clear behavioral difference between casual riders and annual members. Casual riders tend to take longer rides, while annual members generate more rides with shorter average durations.

These behavioral patterns can help Cyclistic design targeted membership campaigns focused on weekend usage, long-duration casual riders, and seasonal demand.
